🃏 How to Play

Your goal is to move all 52 cards into the four foundations.

🃏 Tableau Build cards downward in number, alternating between red and black.
👑 Empty Column Only a King can be placed into an empty column.
🅰️ Foundation Build each suit upward from Ace → 2 → 3 → ... → King.
🔄 Stock Tap the deck to draw cards. When the stock is empty, tap it again to recycle the waste.
📱 Mobile Tap a card, then tap the place where you want to move it.
🖥️ Desktop Drag and drop cards between piles.
🎯 Moving Groups You can move a valid sequence of face-up cards together.
🏆 Win Move all 52 cards into the four foundations to win.
😵 No More Moves If there are no legal moves remaining, the game ends.
